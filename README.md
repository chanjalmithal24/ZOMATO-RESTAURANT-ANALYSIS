# 🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Zomato restaurant data to uncover insights into restaurant distribution, customer ratings, cuisine trends, pricing categories, and the impact of online delivery and table booking. The analysis is presented through interactive dashboards built using **Tableau** and **Power BI**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze restaurant distribution across cities and countries  
- Understand rating patterns and influencing factors  
- Identify top cuisines and pricing categories  
- Evaluate the impact of online delivery and table booking on ratings  
- Track restaurant growth trends over the years  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Data Cleaning & Preparation**: Excel  
- **Data Analysis**: SQL  
- **Data Visualization**: Tableau, Power BI  

---

## 📊 Key Metrics
- **Total Restaurants**: 9,551  
- **Average Rating**: 2.89  
- **Total Cuisines**: 1,826  
- **Total Votes**: ~1 Million  

---

## 📈 Dashboards & Insights

### 🔹 Restaurant Distribution
- Highest concentration of restaurants is in **India**, with **New Delhi, Gurgaon, and Noida** leading among cities.
- Restaurant count shows a steady growth trend from **2010 to 2018**.

### 🔹 Ratings Analysis
- Restaurants with **table booking facility** have a higher average rating (**3.5**) compared to those without (**2.8**).
- Rating categories include **Poor, Average, Good, Very Good, and Excellent**.

### 🔹 Cuisine Insights
- **North Indian cuisine** is the most popular.
- Other top cuisines include **Chinese, Fast Food, Cafe, and Bakery**.

### 🔹 Online Delivery Impact
- **74.3%** of restaurants do **not** offer online delivery.
- Restaurants offering online delivery account for **25.7%**, indicating growth potential.

### 🔹 Pricing Categories
- Restaurants are categorized into **Low, Medium, High, and Luxury** price ranges for better affordability analysis.
